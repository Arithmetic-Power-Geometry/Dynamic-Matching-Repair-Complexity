# Structural-regime experiment\n\nThis experiment is designed to falsify, not merely illustrate, the H(x) hypothesis.\n\nIt compares disjoint hubs, shared hubs, and a regular bounded-degree control while sweeping T. It records mean/max H(x), total work, median, p95, and maximum repair work.\n\n## Prediction\nIf H(x) is mechanistically useful, configurations with similar high degree but lower H(x) should permit lower propagation cost. If degree alone explains performance, the H(x) hypothesis is weakened.\n\n## Threshold selection\nThe Pareto analyzer reports T_total (minimum total work), T_tail (minimum maximum-update work), and nondominated thresholds in the total-work versus maximum-work plane. This prevents choosing T only after observing the favorable metric.\n
