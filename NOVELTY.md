# Contribution and Scope

The paper's contribution is a **repair-centered information perspective** on fully dynamic maximal matching.

## Established contributions

1. **Discovery–propagation separation.** Repair discovery and propagation are charged separately.
2. **Exact explicit-summary theorem.** In the stated restricted model, **P + D ≥ r**.
3. **One-shot TMMR reduction.** Multiphase set disjointness is encoded as a triggered repair question, yielding a conditional polynomial barrier within the one-shot output-stable model.
4. **Future repair burden.** The exact identity **Q = k[AG + Cov(a,g)]** separates recurrence, scan intensity, degree evolution, and interaction.
5. **Negative empirical boundary.** Neighborhood change is not a reliable stand-alone proxy for repair discovery after degree control on the frozen AS-CAIDA trajectory.
6. **Reproducible instrumentation.** Dirty-shadow instrumentation is differentially validated event-by-event on 195,000 updates.

## Explicit non-claims

The work does not claim:
- a new unrestricted fully dynamic maximal-matching update-time bound;
- a general cell-probe lower bound from **P + D ≥ r**;
- a reusable SetIntersection reduction;
- that heavy/light maintenance universally improves total work;
- that repair recurrence alone implies profitable indexing;
- that the future-burden identity is a new complexity lower bound.

These boundaries are part of the contribution and should be preserved when reusing repository results.
