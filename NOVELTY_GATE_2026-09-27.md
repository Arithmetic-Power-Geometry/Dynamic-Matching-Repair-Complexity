# Research Gate — 27 September 2026

This file records the final scope decisions reflected in the V1 paper.

## Retained

- repair discovery versus propagation as the central information-allocation question;
- exact explicit-summary tradeoff **P + D ≥ r**;
- one-shot Multiphase-to-TMMR conditional-hardness reduction;
- exact future-burden decomposition **Q = k[AG + Cov(a,g)]**;
- reproducible real-trace characterization;
- negative AS-CAIDA result after degree control;
- reusable-trigger audit as a scope check.

## Excluded as central novelty

- generic rent-versus-buy or adaptive indexing;
- heavy/light decomposition itself;
- lazy-versus-eager maintenance itself;
- generic dynamic set-intersection witness search;
- recurrence alone as an indexability criterion;
- neighborhood churn alone as a universal predictor of repair difficulty;
- reusable KPP-style lower-bound transfer to unrestricted matching.

The repository and paper should be interpreted using these final boundaries.
